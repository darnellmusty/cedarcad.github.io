CedarCAD Alpha 0.1

Segmented Turning Studio • browser-only prototype • based on v10.5

What to test (10–15 minutes)

  1. Open the turning studio and rotate the finished 3D bowl.
  2. Change segments per ring (try 8, 12, and 16). Check Finished, Glue-up, and Exploded views.
  3. Change base/accent wood species and look for missing updates.
  4. Change the vessel size or profile; check the ring plan reflects your changes.
  5. Open Materials & cut list and Saw setup. Note any dead buttons.
  6. Save a project, reopen it, and check whether your edits survived.

What is NOT ready

The app is intended for exploratory testing. Complex diamond fabrication instructions, material optimization, and production saw setups have not been shop-validated. Please don't use this alpha as the sole source for cutting expensive stock.
