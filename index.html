Recommended construction sequence
  1. Prepare straight, square, dimensioned stock at the specified radial thickness.
  2. Cut each labeled face polygon and maintain full radial depth. Match each piece to the scaled layout before gluing.
  3. Dry-fit and glue the mosaic into a rectangular billet with square top, bottom and outside face.
  4. After curing, cut the billet into the ring’s radial trapezoid wedge using the listed miter angle; dry-fit all units before glue-up.
  5. Glue units into a complete ring, flatten the faces, stack the rings, and turn the final profile, preserving the planned allowances.

CEDARCAD QUALITY TEST · SIMPLE LAMINATED RING

Walnut · Maple · Walnut

This is our easier test pattern. Three straight layers make one laminated blank, which is cut into ordinary trapezoidal wedges. There are no internal diamond angles.

WALNUT
MAPLE ACCENT
WALNUT
  1. Mill three straight, compatible strips with the grain lengthwise: walnut, maple, walnut. Use the indicated rough layer heights and adequate lengths for workholding and setup.
  2. Glue the strips into a single laminated rectangular blank, and let it cure. Square/reference the blank as needed.
  3. Use the normal ring wedge settings to make the trapezoids; the maple stripe travels across the outside face. Test-fit the complete ring before gluing.
  4. Assemble the ring, flatten its faces, then check that the designed profile can be turned from the rough blank.

This is a dimensional test project, not a shop-certified cutting procedure. Extra stock for starter cuts, squaring, and holding fixtures is required. The estimated continuous-run kerf budget excludes those items; test your sled on scrap first.
